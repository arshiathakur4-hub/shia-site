      Currently reading
      

        The Familiar
        by Leigh Bardugo
      

      
      

      
I read Six of Crows, and now I will read every book by her, because she is the one.

      
Opinions so far: I've always loved how she writes and how she talks. I'm personally an atheist, but more than believing in God, I've believed in the idea of things going wrong, because that's how the world is. There's always some kind of wrong pulling in. I have opinions on religion and on higher powers, and they might not be the conventional ones, but I like thinking they aren't all good. She gets me into that headspace.
